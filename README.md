# 🎄 Is That Safe? A Christmas checker for dogs

A tiny single-page tool: tap a holiday thing (tree needles, tinsel, poinsettia, salt-dough ornaments, fruitcake, chocolate, batteries...) and get a quick **✅ okay / ⚠️ careful / ❌ no** answer for dogs. Safe treats go into a little dog-safe stocking you can copy and share.

**Try it:** http://imcharliethedog.com/pages/christmas/

- One `index.html`, no build step, no tracking libraries, works offline once loaded.
- Data lives in the `F` array at the top of the script: `[emoji, name, ok|care|no, explanation]`. Easy to edit, translate or extend.
- Sister projects: [can-dogs-eat](https://github.com/imcharliethedog/can-dogs-eat) (open JSON food list), [dog-advent-calendar](https://github.com/imcharliethedog/dog-advent-calendar).

Corrections and additions are welcome as issues or PRs, especially with a source.

**Not veterinary advice.** General info only; every dog is different. If your dog ate something worrying, call your vet or an emergency line (US: ASPCA Animal Poison Control (888) 426-4435, Pet Poison Helpline (855) 764-7661; fees may apply).
